Terramechanics
==============

A rigid wheel on deformable soil, with the ``bekker_wong`` tire model.

* ``wheel_test_bed.json``: a single-wheel test bed, after the small wheel and TRI-1 lunar soil simulant of P.G. Kumar and S. Jayalekshmi (J. Theoretical and Applied Mechanics 57(3), 2019).
* ``wheel_test_bed_camber.json``: the same test bed with the wheel tilted.

See the `soil <https://shift-dynamics.github.io/shift/force-modules/soil.html>`_ and `tire <https://shift-dynamics.github.io/shift/force-modules/tire.html>`_ pages of the documentation. Other soil models are ``examples/force_modules/dozer_blade.json`` and ``examples/cad/track_frame_soil.json``.
