Screw and gear joints
=====================

``screw_and_gear.json``, written by ``make_screw_and_gear.py``, shows the screw and gear joints.

* A train of four spur gears on fixed axes, of 16, 24, 12 and 36 teeth. Each turns on a revolute joint, and a gear joint between each pair in mesh closes a loop, so the train has one degree of freedom.
* A rod with a right-handed thread and one with a left-handed thread, each on a screw joint to a nut. The pitch of a screw joint is its advance per radian.

See the `model file <https://shift-dynamics.github.io/shift/model-file/model-file.html>`_ page of the documentation.
