Changes: each of the 47 parts was reduced to about 2% of its triangles (2.2
million to 59 thousand), the engine was turned to the axes of shift (x
forward along the crankshaft, z down) and centered on its block, and the
white material was made a dark grey. See scripts/prepare_v8_engine.py. The
source model is commercially usable with this credit.
