Strandbeest
-----------

Theo Jansen's Strandbeests walk on legs of an eight-link planar linkage (with the crank and the frame) whose proportions, his "holy numbers", give the foot a long, flat stride and a quick, high return. Each leg has six moving links and nine revolute joints besides the crank: three loops, which leave the leg no freedom of its own, so one crank drives it.

``strandbeest.json``, generated by ``make_strandbeest.py``, is a walker of twelve of these legs, with Jansen's link lengths in centimeters. Six stations along one crankshaft each carry a front leg and its mirror image, a rear leg, on the same crank pin. The left stations are 120 degrees apart, and so are the right ones, 180 degrees from the left. A PID controller drives the crankshaft. The chassis is free to move, and only the feet, spheres, touch the ground plane. The body stays level as it walks, the property the holy numbers were chosen for.

The links are bones: surfaces of revolution with knobs at the pins and a little irregularity. They are textured by triplanar projection with the color, roughness and normal maps written by ``make_bone_textures.py``.

Regenerate the textures and the model from this directory::

  python3 make_bone_textures.py
  python3 make_strandbeest.py
